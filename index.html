Strategies that can apply in the years your income drops.

Roth conversion

Moving pre-tax retirement savings to a Roth account can make sense in a year your income, and your tax rate, is low.

Still have a question? Contact us
How can I save money on taxes when I'm earning less or no money?

When you're earning a lower income it often means a lower tax bracket, which can open up strategies like Roth Conversion taxed at a lower rate, or selling investment gains at the 0% federal capital gains tax. Retriever models which of these could apply, based on the information you enter, and their estimated value.

Do I have to pay for this?

Retriever is free to use today.

Is this tax advice?

No, nothing on this site is tax advice. Retriever shows you modeled scenarios based on the information you enter.

How accurate are these numbers? What are they based on?

All of these numbers are calculated by a deterministic optimization engine that reads federal and state tax codes and considers thousands of potential decision points for the financial context you enter. The numbers are estimates based on the information you provide and current tax law. Retriever doesn't see your full tax situation, so treat the output as a starting point to discuss with a professional, not a final answer.

Is this a competitor to TurboTax, FreeTaxUSA, or Sprintax?

No, Retriever models tax savings strategies for before you file your taxes. It does not file for you, so you can continue to use any service to file taxes.

Who built this? Are you tax professionals?

No. Retriever was built by a Columbia MBA/MS candidate ('27) with 5 years building financial products at a fintech, after realizing how many tax strategies go unused by his classmates. No one on the team is a CPA or tax preparer.

Does this work if I'm not a US citizen?

Retriever models federal and state taxes for students filing as US taxpayers. Talk to a professional who handles international student taxes.

What is the estimated savings counter?

The sum of each user's latest estimated savings modeled by Retriever, based on the information they entered. It updates once a day. The sum is estimates, not necessarily realized savings, and not a guarantee.
